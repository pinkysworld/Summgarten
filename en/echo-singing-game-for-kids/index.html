What is a sing-back game?

One person sings or hums a short phrase and the other sings it back like an echo. It trains careful listening and children often enjoy it quickly because they get an immediate response.

How to play the echo game at home

  1. Start with a single note on “la” or “mmm”.
  2. Sing it calmly and not too high, then give your child a moment.
  3. When that works, try two notes: a high one and a low one.
  4. Swap roles: your child sings first and you sing back.
  5. Stop before interest fades. Short rounds beat long ones.

The aim is not to hit the note exactly. What matters is that your child notices: my voice is heard and answered.

Variations

  • Animal echo: One animal calls, the other answers.
  • Echo with movement: High note, arms up; low note, crouch down.
  • Whisper echo: Loud first, then back quietly.
  • Rhythm echo: Clap instead of sing.

The sing-back game in Hum Garden

Hum Garden (originally Summgarten) is a music game for ages 2–6. In the garden, a sung note makes a little creature grow and sing it back. There is also an echo game in which children answer the creatures. With a microphone the child's own voice is used; without one, 12 built-in sounds are available.

Recordings are stored only locally on the device so the creatures can sing them back, and can be deleted in the parents' area. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and works offline. It does not replace an echo game with you, but it complements one when you play together. See also: how to get kids to sing.

FAQ

Does my child have to hit the note exactly?

No. It is about listening and joining in, not perfect pitch.

What happens to my child's voice in the app?

Recordings stay on the device and can be deleted in the parents' area.

Does the game work without a microphone?

Yes. There are 12 built-in sounds.
